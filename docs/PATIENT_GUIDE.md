# Patient User Guide — JeevanCare

Welcome to JeevanCare, your private and secure digital health companion.

---

## 1. Getting Started

1. **Creating an Account**: Click **Sign Up**, enter your email address, create a password, and verify your account.
2. **Demo Mode**: If you wish to explore the platform before creating an account, click **Explore Demo**.

---

## 2. Main Features

### 2.1 The Health Dashboard
- View your upcoming medication doses for the day.
- Check your scheduled doctor appointments.
- Review your vital trends (Blood Pressure, Sugar levels).

### 2.2 Scanning Prescriptions
1. Open the **Prescription Scanner** from the navigation menu.
2. Take a photo or upload an image of your prescription.
3. The system will read the doctor's handwriting and show you the extracted medicine names and dosages.
4. Review the details, make any corrections, and click **Save to Active Medications**.

### 2.3 Medical Vault
- Store your lab reports, X-rays, and discharge summaries securely in one place.
- Tag and categorize your files for quick retrieval when visiting a clinic.
- Download or generate PDF summaries of your health records.

### 2.4 Medication Tracking
- Check off medicines as you take them throughout the day.
- View remaining doses and receive alerts when you are running low.

### 2.5 AI Health Assistant
- Ask questions about your medications, symptoms, or lab test results in natural language.
- Speak directly using the voice assistant button in supported languages.
- *Important*: The assistant provides helpful educational guidance but does not replace your doctor's medical judgment.

### 2.6 Finding Nearby Healthcare
- Use the **Nearby Healthcare** tab to locate certified pharmacies, clinics, and hospitals in your vicinity.

---

## 3. Account Privacy & Logout

- When you are finished, click the **Profile** icon and select **Sign Out** to securely clear your private session from the device.
