# Doctor & Clinician Workspace Guide — JeevanCare

This guide explains the features available to licensed medical practitioners within the JeevanCare Doctor Workspace.

---

## 1. Accessing the Doctor Workspace

1. Sign in with an account assigned the **Doctor** role.
2. Select **Doctor Portal** from the header navigation bar.

---

## 2. Workspace Capabilities

### 2.1 Doctor Dashboard
- Review today's appointment schedule.
- Monitor triage queues and critical vitals alerts for monitored patients.

### 2.2 Patient Directory & History
- Access the list of patients under your care.
- View historical vitals, past prescriptions, and uploaded vault reports.

### 2.3 Clinical Notes & Diagnosis
- Author SOAP notes (Subjective, Objective, Assessment, Plan).
- Save clinical observations directly to the patient's record.

### 2.4 E-Prescriptions
- Generate digital prescriptions with standardized dosages and duration schedules.
- Prescriptions automatically sync to the patient's active medication reminders.

---

## 3. Security & Access Rules

- Physicians can only view and manage records for patients with an active consultation or clinical relationship.
- All clinical notes are signed with the doctor's identifier and timestamped for audit compliance.
